How reviews work

This page follows the active tab and includes words replaced there. Due for review shows scheduled cards you previously encountered while browsing. All vocabulary lets you practise any entry. “Again” brings a card back in 10 minutes. “Knew it” advances through 1, 3, 7, 14, 30 and 60 days. Exact duplicate rows share one review card.

Encountered word IDs are saved locally for reviews, without page addresses. Replacements can be below the fold; they do not prove you read the word.

Where you meet words

Optional, on this device only. Saves up to 50 recent hostnames and vocabulary matches. No page text, page paths or private-window history. Matches indicate replacements, not proof you read them.

Explicit grammar coverage

Supported: article + noun, article + adjective + noun, and adjective + noun. Supplied metadata controls gender, number and adjective forms. Every output word must occur in your vocabulary. Unknown forms and preposition + article phrases stay unchanged.

Grammar rules do not resolve every English ambiguity; this remains vocabulary practice.
